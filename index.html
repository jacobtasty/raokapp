How it works

Two minutes a day. No pressure, no competition. Just a little more kindness in the world.

1

Reveal today's act

Every morning, everyone gets the same small act, like holding a door or complimenting a stranger's dog. Tap the card to reveal it.

2

Open your private one

A second act, just for you, gently stretches your comfort zone. Do it, swap it, or skip it. Skipping never breaks your streak.

3

Watch kindness grow

Tick it off and see the live count rise as people across the country do the same. Share a story if you like.
