Why we say that

In 2022, psychologists Amit Kumar and Nicholas Epley ran a series of experiments, like asking people in a park to give a stranger a hot chocolate. Givers then guessed how the recipient felt, and recipients said how they actually felt.

Every time, the people being kind underestimated how good the other person felt. On a mood scale, givers guessed about 2.3, while recipients reported about 3.6.

Kumar, A. & Epley, N. (2023). A little good goes an unexpectedly long way: Underestimating the positive impact of kindness on recipients. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General.
