Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version: RAOK doesn't need your name, email or password to use the app. Adding an email to keep your progress is optional. We only store what's needed to run the shared community features: the days you did the daily act, your town, any story you choose to share and your handprint. Your personal acts, feelings and journal stay on your phone and never reach us. You can delete your data at any time from the app.

Who we are

RAOK (Random Acts of Kindness) is a web app at raokapp.com that encourages small daily acts of kindness. RAOK is the "controller" of your information, which means we decide how it's used. You can contact us about anything in this policy at akindhello@raokapp.com.

What we collect, and why

What stays on your phone

Some things are stored only in your phone's browser and are never sent to us: your personal acts, how each act felt, your journal, your comfort level, your "why", your streak history and your settings. Clearing your browser data or using "Delete my data" removes them.

Our legal basis

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) we use your information because it's needed to provide the features you choose to use, and because we have a legitimate interest in keeping RAOK safe and improving it. For the waitlist and anything you choose to share, such as stories and feedback, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. We use a small number of trusted services to run RAOK:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and the anonymous accounts.
  • Netlify, which hosts the website and receives the waitlist and feedback forms.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the app's fonts. Your browser connects to Google to download them.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Acts, stories, handprints, app-open days and your email (if you added one) are kept while you use RAOK. If you don't use the app for 2 years, we'll delete them.
  • Reports are kept for up to 1 year after we've dealt with them.
  • Feedback is kept for up to 2 years.
  • Waitlist emails are kept until RAOK launches or you ask us to remove yours.

Age

RAOK is for people aged 16 and over. We ask your age before the app starts, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you think a child has used RAOK, email us and we'll delete their data.

Keeping stories safe

Stories are checked for offensive words, links and phone numbers before they're shared. Anyone can report a story. A story is hidden for you straight away when you report it, and hidden for everyone once more than one person reports it, until the RAOK team has checked it. Please don't name people or share details that could identify anyone, especially children.

Your rights

You have the right to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit how we use it. The quickest way to delete everything is You → Settings → Delete my data in the app. Because RAOK doesn't know your name, if you email us about your data we may ask you to send your request from the app so we can find your account.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if anything changes and show the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app.
